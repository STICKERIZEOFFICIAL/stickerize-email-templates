# Stickerize Rise email subjects and preview text

## Gift card

**Subject line**  
{{sender_first_name}} bought you a gift card at {{business_name}}

**Preview text**  
Put your gift card toward custom stickers made from your own designs.

## Store Credits

**Subject line**  
{{sender_first_name}}, your cash back is in your account

**Preview text**  
Use your Stickerize credit on your next order whenever you’re ready.

## Refund

**Subject line**  
Your store credit for order: {{order_number}}

**Preview text**  
Your refund was issued as Stickerize store credit. You can use it on a future order.

## Cashback

**Subject line**  
You got cashback from your last order

**Preview text**  
Your reward is ready to put toward your next batch of custom stickers.

## Compensation

**Subject line**

**Subject line**
